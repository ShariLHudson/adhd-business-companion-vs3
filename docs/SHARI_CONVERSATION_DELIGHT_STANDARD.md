# Shari Conversation Delight Standard

**Runtime SoT:** `lib/shariAnswerFirst/conversationDelight.ts`  
**Combined with excellence:** `validateConversationExcellence`  
**Not entertainment** — meaningful usefulness.

## Signals of delight

Members should be able to feel at least one of:

- It remembered me  
- It understood what I needed  
- It saved me work  
- I had not thought of that  
- I know what to do now  
- This feels manageable  
- That recommendation makes sense  
- This is better than a generic answer  

## Dimensions (0–10)

Felt understood · saved effort · practical surprise · confidence created · personalization impact · memorable insight · emotional fit · preference over generic AI

`passes` when average ≥ ~6.5 and not weaker than general-AI baseline / category-menu openings.

## Repair

Delight failures produce targeted repair instructions (insight, shortcut, personalized application, judgment, etc.) used by model repair before local fail-safes.
